ABOUT THIS GUIDE

1THINGS TO DO

In Messed Up Dress Up, your friend can do things, not just walk around. They can open treasure chests, pick things up, carry them in a backpack, put them down somewhere new, lift pots and throw them, swing a bug net, and press floor switches.

This guide shows you how. There are puzzles to solve, and a whole dungeon to explore!

2THE ACTION BUTTON

When your friend is close to something they can use, it glows, like a door does. Then press the action button:

  • On a keyboard: Enter.
  • On a game controller: the 🫳 button (the same one that goes through a glowing door).
  • No keyboard? Tap it (or click it). Your friend walks over and does it.

When you point at it, it says what will happen: 💰 open the chest, 🎒 pick it up...

10TOOLS: HOLD AND SWING

A tool is something you use with your hands. The first tool is the 🥅 bug net.

  1. Find the bug net and put it in your backpack.
  2. On its card, press ✋ hold it. Now your friend holds the net.
  3. Press U on a keyboard, or Y on a controller, to swing it. (No keyboard? Tap a bee.)

11FLOOR SWITCHES

A floor switch is a stone in the floor that can be pressed down. When it is down, it glows gold. There are two kinds:

  • 🔁 Step-on switches go down when you step on them, and up again the next time you step on them.
  • ⬇️ Hold-down switches are only down while something is on them. Stand on one, and it goes down. Step off, and it comes up again! To keep it down, put something on it: a 🪨 rock, or a 🗿 statue.

Some hold-down switches have a picture on them. Only the thing in the picture can hold that one down. Walk up to it and press the action button: if that thing is in your backpack, your friend puts it on the switch. If not, they tell you what it wants.

WHAT DO SWITCHES DO?

A switch can open a way out of a room, open trap doors, or make something appear. In 🏞️ Background, a floor switch is in 🎚️ switches and traps, and you choose what it does.

CATCH A BEE 🐝

Can you catch a bee? You need two things: a 🥅 bug net to catch it, and an empty 🍾 glass bottle to keep it in. Where could they be? (A hint: one is up in the trees, and the other is with someone who makes medicine.) Then find a bee, hold the net, and swing!

THE FLOOR SWITCH PUZZLE

In the 🎒 Backpack tab, press 🧩 put the floor switch puzzle here. Chests appear, and floor switches with pictures. Can you get all the switches down at once?

13THE DUNGEON 🗝️

Deep under the world there is a dungeon: a maze of 64 stone rooms. Somewhere in the start world there is a way down. Somewhere in the dungeon there is a treasure room... and a way back out.

Some walls in the dungeon are gates. A floor switch opens each one. Look around each room: what could press it?

CATCHING A BEE: THE ANSWER

  1. Go to the tree house (inside, up the ladder). Pick up the 🥅 bug net.
  2. Go to the healer's hut. Pick up the 🍾 glass bottle.
  3. In the 🎒 tab, press ✋ hold it on the net's card.
  4. Go to the clearing or a forest, walk up to a 🐝 bee, and swing (U, Y, or tap the bee).
  5. The bee goes into the bottle, the cork goes on, pop!, and the bottle goes into your backpack.

Swing without an empty bottle and the bee just buzzes away. To set it free, press 🐝 let it go on its card.

THE FLOOR SWITCH PUZZLE: THE ANSWER

Open the three chests: they give a 👑 crown, a 🐚 shell and a 🗡️ sword. Put each one on the switch with its picture (walk up to the switch and press the action button). The fourth switch takes anything: pick up the 🗿 statue and put it there. When every switch is down, click, click, click! A new chest appears with a surprise inside: a 🥚 dragon egg!
