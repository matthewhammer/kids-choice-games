1WHAT IS A BACKPACK?

A backpack is a bag you wear on your back. You can put things in it and carry them with you.

In Messed Up Dress Up, your friend has a backpack too. When your friend finds a treasure, it goes into the backpack. Later, you can take it out and put it down somewhere new.

GOOD TO KNOW

The backpack goes with your friend everywhere: to the farm, to the castle, to the sea. What is in it stays in it.

2PUT IT ON

At first, you can't see the backpack. Let's put it on!

  1. Open the side panel.
  2. Choose the 🎒 Backpack tab.
  3. Press 🎒 drawn on the friend.

Now your friend wears it. Press it again to take it off. The things inside stay inside.

3FIND A TREASURE CHEST

A treasure chest 💰 is a box with a lid. A chest that is shut has a surprise in it.

The dungeon rooms have chests, and you can put one anywhere you like:

  1. Open the 🏞️ Background tab.
  2. Find 🪨 things to put here, and choose 💰 treasure chest.
  3. Pick what comes out, in 🎁 what comes out.

📷 a picture of this is coming

4OPEN IT!

  1. Walk your friend right up to the chest.
  2. Press Enter on the keyboard (on a game controller, the button that goes through doors).

The lid opens. The treasure flies up over your friend's head, and you hear music. Then the treasure goes into the backpack!

TRY THIS

You can record your own sound for a chest in the 🎙️ Recordings tab, and pick it for the chest. Then the chest plays your voice when it opens!

5LOOK INSIDE

Open the 🎒 Backpack tab again. Under 🎒 what's in it, each thing has a card: its picture, its name, and when you found it.

9THE PUZZLE ROOM 🧩

COMING SOON

This part of the backpack is being built right now. When it is in the game, this page will show it with pictures.

In the puzzle room there are three stone plates on the floor. Each plate has a picture on it: a 👑 crown, a 🐚 shell, and a 🗡️ sword.

There are three shut chests, too. Can you open the chests, and put each treasure on the plate that shows it?

When all three plates have the right thing on them... something happens! What is it? Play and find out.
