1WHAT'S NEW

You can fly now. Get in the 🎈 hot-air balloon, or ride a 🫧 big bubble, and the game stops showing one place at a time from the side. It shows the whole world from above: every square of the world is a patch of a quilt, with its paths running on into the next square's, so you see how the places join up.

Every place is drawn from above the way its own scene draws it: the lake's ducks, the castle's moat and towers, the tree houses on their tree, the witch's hut behind its fence of skulls. Every thing you have placed in a square is there too, drawn from above: trees, rocks, chests, animals, cacti.

TRY IT

Open 🎒 (or press I), find 🎈 the hot-air balloon, and tap 🛫 fly.

2TAKE OFF 🎈

The two crafts are items, like 🫧 blowing bubbles and 🔥 breathing fire: anyone has them. Tap 🛫 fly on one in 🎒 Your items, or put it in 🔹 or 🔸 and press that button (Z or X on the keyboard, the triggers on a controller).

You take off from the square you are in. Inside a house or a cave there is no sky, so the game says to go outside first.

3HOW HIGH ⬆️⬇️

How high you are is how much of the world you see. Low down, one square fills the picture and you see its small things. Higher, the squares around it come into view. At the top you see the whole world, with clouds drifting between you and the ground.

4LANDING 🛬

Sink all the way down over a place, or press 🛬, and you land in that place, in its usual view. Over an empty square there is nowhere to land: the game says so and you stay up. While you fly, the game's own buttons rest (no fire, no bubbles, no tools): you are up in the sky.

5EVERY PLACE FROM ABOVE

Here is every place, each one by itself in a square, on the left as you see it when you are there and on the right from above. Up there, its paths go to the edges where its ways lead, and they meet its neighbours' paths. The small things, like flowers, ducks and toys, are only drawn when you fly low.

6PLACED THINGS

A thing you put in a square with 🏞️ Background stays in that square, and from above it is drawn where you put it: across the square where it stands across the scene, and nearer the bottom the nearer it stands. Here are squares of the friend's own world with things placed in them.

PUT ONE AND LOOK

Put a cow or a tree in the square you are in, then take off: it is there, below you.

8IS IT FAST?

The game's first rule is 60 frames a second for everything a controller does. Flying keeps to it by drawing the quilt once, when you take off, and then only moving and scaling it: one change a frame, whatever the height. The squares out of view are hidden, and high up each square's small things are left out.
