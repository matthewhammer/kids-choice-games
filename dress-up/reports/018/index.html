1YOU ARE THE DIRECTOR

In a film, the director tells the actors what to do: walk over there, turn around, say your line, dance! In Messed Up Dress Up, you are the director, and your friend is the actor.

You write what happens as a story script: a list of steps, one step on each line. Your friend does them in order, from the top line to the bottom line.

walk 30 60
say Hello!
pose dance

This story has three steps. Your friend walks, says “Hello!”, and dances. That's it! You just wrote your first story.

★ GOOD TO KNOW

Each place has its own story. The farm yard can have one story, the castle another, and the tree house another.

2WHERE THE STORY BOX IS

  1. Open the side panel and choose the 🏞️ Background tab.
  2. Find the 🎬 a story for this place box.
  3. Write your steps in it, one on each line.
  4. Press ▶ play. Your friend acts it out!

[picture 01-story-box.png not taken]

Three buttons:

  • ▶ play starts the story from the top. If the box is empty, it plays an example story.
  • ⏹ stop stops it.
  • 🎬 play when we come here: when it is on, the story starts by itself every time you come into this place.
★ YOU CAN ALWAYS TAKE OVER

Click or tap in the picture, or press a key, and the story stops so you can play yourself.

3THE SEVEN STEPS

A story knows seven steps. Each line starts with one of these words:

stepwhat your friend doestry it
walkwalks to a spot in the picture (chapter 4 says how spots work)walk 30 60
waitwaits, a number of secondswait 2
posestands, sits, lies down, meditates, hugs, or dancespose dance
faceturns to look left, right, front (at you), or backface right
pullwalks to a lever and pulls itpull L
saysays some words, shown over the picturesay Hello!
loopgoes back to the top and does it all againloop

The poses are: stand, sit, lie, meditate, hug and dance. Each dance is a surprise: your friend picks a different one every time.

A line that starts with # is a note for you. Your friend skips it.

# this is a note: my friend does not read it
say Hi!

4WHERE IS 30? WHERE IS 60?

walk takes two numbers. The first says how far across the picture: 0 is the left edge, 50 the middle, 100 the right edge. The second says how far toward you: 0 is far away at the back, 100 is right at the front.

25
50
75
back: 0
front: 100
walk 30 60
walk 75 40
walk 10 100

You can leave out the second number: walk 80 walks to 80 across and stays just as near or far as before.

★ TRY THIS

walk 0, then walk 100. Your friend walks all the way across.

5YOUR FIRST STORY

Go to the farm yard and type this story in the box:

# a walk round the farm yard
walk 30 60
face right
say Good morning, farm!
pose dance
wait 2
pose stand
walk 75 40
face front
say Time for a rest.
pose sit

Press ▶ play. Your friend walks out, turns, says good morning, dances, walks to the back, and sits down for a rest.

[picture 04-sit.png not taken]

Change one line and play it again. Make your friend say something else. Make them dance twice. It's your story!

6PULLING LEVERS

pull makes your friend walk to a lever and pull it, just like when you click a lever yourself.

  • In the castle's trap room: pull L and pull R for the red levers, pull BL and pull BR for the blue ones.
  • In a tree room: pull H for the trap door's lever.
  • Levers you put in a place yourself have numbers: pull 1 is the first one, pull 2 the second.
walk 25 55
say Which lever opens the pit?
pull L
wait 1
face front
say That one!

[picture 05-lever.png not taken]

⚠ CAREFUL

A pit that opens under your friend is a pit they fall into. That can be the story, too!

7AGAIN AND AGAIN: LOOP

Put loop at the end and the story starts over from the top, forever, until you press ⏹ stop or take over.

# the castle guard
walk 20 50
face right
wait 2
walk 80 50
face left
wait 2
loop

8EVERYONE CAN WALK 🚶

Your friend isn't the only one who can move. The other people you made in the 👥 People tab can walk too, each on their own path.

  1. Put a person in a place with 📍 here.
  2. Press 🚶 path next to their name.
  3. Write their path in the box, with the same steps as a story: walk, wait, pose, face.
  4. Press ▶ walk it.

[picture 06-people-path.png not taken]

A path always goes round and round: when it gets to the end, it starts again by itself.

[picture 07-walkers.png not taken]

★ GOOD TO KNOW

People on paths can't say things or pull levers. Only your friend's story can do that.

9OOPS! A WRONG LINE

If a line has a word the story doesn't know, the story stops at that line and tells you which line it is, and what it can do instead.

walk 30 60
jump 5
say I never get here

[picture 08-wrong-line.png not taken]

The story box said: (the run did not record it)

Things that go wrong, and how to fix them:

  • A spelling slip, like wlak: fix the word.
  • A missing number, like walk on its own or wait with no seconds: add the number.
  • A pose it doesn't know, like pose jump: use stand, sit, lie, meditate, hug or dance.
  • A lever that isn't here, like pull L in the farm yard: go to the trap room, or pull a lever you placed.
  • It stopped by itself? Maybe you clicked the picture. Press ▶ play again.

11THE QUICK CARD

walk X Z: to X across (0–100), Z toward you (0–100)

wait S: S seconds

pose …: stand, sit, lie, meditate, hug, dance

face …: left, right, front, back

pull …: L, R, BL, BR, H, or a lever's number

say …: words over the picture

loop: back to the top

# …: a note, skipped

▶ play · ⏹ stop · 🎬 play when we come here

🚶 paths: walk, wait, pose, face; always loop
