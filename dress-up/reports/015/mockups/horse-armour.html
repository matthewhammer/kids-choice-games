How it could go into the game

  • In 🧍 Body's 🐴 the horse, one more chooser, "🛡️ its armour": none, or one of these six.
  • The cloth and the plume take the knight's colours and emblem from 🐯 Costume. If the friend is not a knight, they take the horse's halter colour. It's one place to choose and the two always match.
  • Each piece is its own drawing from the side, the front and behind, like the manes. Ridden, the cloth goes under the saddle.
  • The pieces could also be placed on their own, like the helmet in the trap room: a 🐴 chanfron on a stand in the armoury.

Ideas from LEGO Castle: the horse battle helmet with a plume, in Knights' Kingdom (6095), gave the 🧱 set. The cloth barding of the Lion Knights' Castle (10305) gave the half caparison. The Dragon Knights' dragon-head armour gave the 🐉 helm, to go with the dragon costume (ticket 499). The real pieces' names: chanfron (face), crinet (neck), peytral (chest), crupper (rump), flanchards (sides), caparison (the cloth).
