Six kinds of fire

How it could go into the game

  • 🐉 dragon joins the animal suits in 👗 Costume (tiger, wolf, bear…). It has the suit's hood and paws. It adds horns, a row of spines down the hood, teeth along the hood's edge, slit eyes on the hood, scales, a plated belly, claws, bat wings and a long tail with spines and a spade at the end.
  • One colour chooser each: its scales, its belly, its wings and spines, its horns, teeth and claws.
  • 🔥 breathe fire is an action like 🫧 blowing bubbles: a button on the screen and on the controller. Hold it for a stream; tap it for a puff. It comes "from" the 👄 mouth, the 🫲 left hand or the 🫱 right hand, as bubbles do, so a wizard or a knight's torch could use it too.
  • The six kinds are fire makers that anything can use. A torch, a campfire, a cannon, or a dragon you place in a scene could each shoot one.
  • Every fire here is a fixed set of shapes moved by the browser's own animation. The game does no work per frame for it, which keeps the 60 frames a second.

Ideas from LEGO Castle: the translucent orange "dragon's fire" piece and the three-pointed flame plume in the Dragon Knights battle pack (2010) gave the 🧱 brick fire. The Dragon Suit Guy (Collectible Minifigures, 2018) is the same idea as this costume: a person in a dragon suit, face showing.
