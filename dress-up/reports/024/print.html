2A SWORD THAT CUTS

A sword can cut down plants. First, your friend needs a sword in their hand. There are three ways:

  • Dress up as a knight or as the Time Wanderer in 🐯 Costume. They have their own sword.
  • Find the 🗡️ sword in the castle's smithy, pick it up, then press ✋ hold it on its card in the 🎒 Backpack tab.
  • In 🧍 Body, tap 🫱 right hand holds (or 🫲 left) and choose 🗡️ sword.

Now walk up to a plant, face it, and swing: press U on a keyboard, or the controller's 🥅 button (the one that swings the bug net).

The nearest plant in front of your friend goes in a puff of leaves. One plant each swing. A sword cuts trees, bushes, vines, cactuses, clumps of flowers and mushrooms.

GOOD TO KNOW

Rows of flowers and grass that go all the way across a place are not cut. They are there to walk through.

3WHAT IS CUT STAYS CUT

A plant you cut is gone, and it is not in your way any more. In the 🌍 world, every square remembers its own cut plants: cut a tree in one forest, and the forest next door still has all of its trees.

Want one back right now? Open 🏞️ Background and look at 👁️ default background objects: everything the place draws itself, a chip each. A cut plant's chip shows 🙈. Tap it, and the plant grows back.

Plants can also grow back by themselves. See chapter 6.

4NIGHT AND DAY 🌗

Now the game can have time. It is off to start. To turn it on:

  1. Open the ⚙️ Advanced tab.
  2. Press 🌗 night and day: night falls, and you sleep in a bed till morning.

Now the hour shows at the top of the picture: ☀️ in the daytime, 🌙 at night. A whole day takes 12 minutes to start. Change it with ⏱️.

After sunset, every place grows dark (less so inside). The game tells you it is bedtime:

🌙 Night is falling. Find somewhere to sleep: a bed, a bunk, a sleeping bag or a blanket.

Some places have their own night, with the moon and the stars: the jungle waterfall, the cave, the camp site, the tent and the fairy forest.

GOOD TO KNOW

The hour and the date come from 🌍 where and when, so the sun and the moon are where they really would be.

5TIME FOR BED 🛌

When it is dark, find a bed: the bed in the grass hut's bedroom, a bunk, the sleeping bags in a tent, or a blanket. Tap the bed (or click it), and your friend walks over and lies down.

Your friend falls asleep. It goes dark, and then it is 7 in the morning, the next day:

🌅 Good morning! Nights slept: 1.
  • 🛌 counts the nights you have slept. It shows at the top of the picture, and in ⚙️ Advanced.
  • Lying down in the daytime is only a rest: “It's still daytime. Come back to bed when it's dark.”
  • Stay up all night if you like. Morning comes anyway!

6PLANTS GROW BACK 🌱

This was M's idea too. Plants you cut with the sword come back by themselves. It is on to start. You will find it in ⚙️ Advanced, under 🌗 night and day:

  • A cut plant grows back after 2 minutes. Change it with ⏱️, from 1 to 30 minutes.
  • It comes back with a puff of leaves and a 🌱, even while you are somewhere else.
  • With 🌗 night and day on, cut plants grow back by the next morning instead. Sleep in a bed, and everything you cut is back!
  • A bush or a cactus that you put in a place grows back too, right where it was.

Don't want them back? Press 🌱 cut plants grow back again to turn it off. Then what you cut stays cut.

7THE GARDEN 🌻

A brand new place, made for M's garden. It is one of the 🏠 home places. To go there, open 🏞️ Background, choose 🏠 home, then 🌻 garden:

In the garden there is:

  • a white picket fence with an arbor of climbing roses, and snowy Colorado mountains far away;
  • tall flowers along the fence;
  • a garden shed with a window box, and a rain barrel;
  • an apple tree, a bench, and a red wheelbarrow;
  • 🐝 bees (catch one with the bug net: ^du_report(23) shows how);
  • beds of dug soil, either side of a path of stepping stones, ready for planting (chapter 9 shows what to plant).

How many beds? In 🏞️ Background, 🟫 beds of soil to plant in: from none to six.

PUT IT IN YOUR WORLD

In 🗺️ World design, drag 🌻 garden (or 💧 spring) onto a square of the grid. Then you can walk to it from the squares next door.

8THE SPRING 💧

The other new place is the spring, one of the 💧 water places. It is like the lake, but small, with land all the way round: a little round pool, bubbling up in a meadow, with woods behind it.

Walk right into it! Your friend wades in the water.

Look closely: there are frogs on the stones and lily pads, puffing their throats in and out, and bubbles coming up in the middle, where the water wells up from under the ground.

To go there, open 🏞️ Background and choose 💧 water, then 💧 spring. Its settings are there too: 🐸 frogs (none to three) and 🫧 how much it bubbles.

9PLANT A GARDEN 🌷

Now the garden has things to plant! Open 🏞️ Background, find 🪨 things to put here, and tap 🌻 garden. There are two:

Tap + 🌷 flower bed or + 🥕 vegetable patch, or drag one into the picture, right where you want it to grow. Drag it again to move it.

Each one you put gets its own card, below the buttons. On a flower bed's card, choose 🌼 which flower. A vegetable patch's card has 🥕 which vegetable.

There are eight flowers, and they all grow wild in Colorado:

  • 💙 Colorado blue columbine (Colorado's own flower)
  • 🖌️ Indian paintbrush
  • 🔔 Rocky Mountain penstemon
  • 🌞 blanket flower
  • 🌻 sunflowers
  • 💜 silvery lupine
  • 🎩 prairie coneflower (it looks like a Mexican hat)
  • 🩵 wild blue flax

And there are eight vegetables that grow in the United States:

  • 🍅 tomatoes, in their cage
  • 🌽 corn
  • 🥕 carrots
  • 🎃 pumpkins
  • 🥬 lettuce
  • 🫛 peas, climbing up their stakes
  • 🫑 bell peppers
  • 🥦 broccoli

Slide 🌱 how grown on a plant's card to make it small or big. A plant has four stages:

  1. Sprouts, just poking out of the soil.
  2. Young plants, green all over.
  3. In bud, with the fruit still green.
  4. In flower, and the vegetables ripe.
GOOD TO KNOW

New plants start fully grown. You can walk right through them, and the 🗡️ sword does not cut them.

10COMING SOON

The garden is not finished yet! Next, a 💧 watering can, hidden in the tree house. Fill it at the spring, the lake or the castle's moat, then water your plants. Each plant will show how much water it has, and grow when it is watered.

11FIXED

Some things were broken. Now they work:

  • One dungeon. The game was making a new 🗝️ dungeon every time the page loaded, with more and more holes down to it in the cemetery and the castle. Now a game keeps just one. 0.0.468
  • Every room has its things. After you went into one room with things to find, the other rooms were empty. Now every room has its own things the first time you go in, so the bug-catching puzzle works whichever room you visit first. 0.0.468
  • 📜 Scripting's Hazel tiles load again. They were stuck at “loading”. 0.0.467
  • Places look the same every time. The witch's room, with its glowing mushroom, was sometimes drawn a tiny bit differently. 0.0.469

12MAKE UP A STORY

Some ideas:

  • A knight cuts a path through the forest... and by morning the forest has grown back!
  • Stay up late at the camp site, look at the stars, then sleep in the tent.
  • Count how many nights your friend can sleep. Can you get to ten?
  • The frogs of the spring are having a party. Who is invited?
  • Catch a bee in the garden, then let it go again by the flowers.
  • Plant a vegetable garden. What will you cook when everything is ripe?
